Farm Tips

Soil Health

Test your soil pH annually and add compost to improve fertility. Rotate crops to prevent nutrient depletion.

Water Management

Use drip irrigation to save water and ensure even distribution. Check weather forecasts to avoid overwatering.

Pest Control

Introduce beneficial insects like ladybugs to naturally reduce pests. Avoid chemical overuse to protect soil.
